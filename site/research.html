M.Sc. thesis, in progress

Deep unfolding for anomaly detection in medical imaging

Data Mining Lab, Iran University of Science and Technology. Supervisors: Prof. Behrouz Minaei-Bidgoli and Dr. Maryam Amoozegar.

Deep unfolding turns the steps of an iterative optimization algorithm into the layers of a neural network. A standard deep network is hard to interpret. An unfolded network keeps the structure of the solver, so its layers stay interpretable.

I am designing a deep unfolding network that detects anomalies in medical imaging modalities such as MRI and CT. I lead the problem formulation, architecture design, PyTorch implementation, and evaluation on medical datasets. Experiments are in progress.

Mar 2026 to present
Manuscript in preparation

Online unsupervised hybrid network for moving object segmentation

Kerman Graduate University of Technology, with Dr. Maryam Amoozegar.

The method starts from IFISTA-ROTAB, an online tensor-based background subtraction method. It splits each video frame into a low-rank background and a sparse foreground.

I replaced the update of the temporal-dependency vector with a ConvLSTM, which is a recurrent network built from convolutions. I replaced the foreground update with a convolutional network. I kept the recursive least squares (RLS) updates of the background subspace factors.

The model is fully online and unsupervised. It processes one frame at a time and trains without ground-truth masks. I redesigned the loss function to give a stronger training signal. I am the sole technical contributor.

Result: the F-measure improves by at least 0.1 over IFISTA-ROTAB on the LASIESTA and CDNet2014 datasets.

Feb 2024 to Jul 2024
B.Sc. project

Heterogeneous graph neural network for Persian sentiment analysis

Shahid Bahonar University of Kerman, with Prof. Mahdi Eftekhari.

We adapted a heterogeneous graph neural network method for text classification, originally developed for English, to Persian. I worked in a team of 3 students.

I rebuilt the text-to-graph pipeline for Persian. It uses fastText word embeddings and Hazm for part-of-speech tagging and dependency parsing. Each review becomes a graph of word, sentence, tag, and dependency nodes. We trained and evaluated a heterogeneous graph attention network on the Digikala and SnappFood review datasets.

Grade: 20/20.
