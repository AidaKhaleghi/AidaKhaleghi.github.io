M.Sc. thesis

Interpretable deep unfolding for anomaly detection in medical imaging

Data Mining Lab, Iran University of Science and Technology · with Prof. Behrouz Minaei-Bidgoli and Dr. Maryam Amoozegar

Deep unfolding turns each iteration of an optimization algorithm into a layer of a neural network. The result keeps the structure, and so the interpretability, of the original solver while learning its parameters from data.

  • Developing an RPCA-based deep unfolding network that separates a medical image into normal structure and sparse anomalies.
  • Designing an architecture that combines convolutional and attention modules to unfold the RPCA optimization.
  • Implementing the method in PyTorch and evaluating it on medical imaging datasets such as BraTS (ongoing).

Online unsupervised hybrid network for moving object segmentation

Kerman Graduate University of Technology · with Dr. Maryam Amoozegar

  • Extended IFISTA-ROTAB, an online tensor-based moving object segmentation method, into a hybrid network.
  • Replaced the temporal and foreground updates with ConvLSTM and CNN modules, while keeping the recursive least squares (RLS) updates of the background factors.
  • Implemented and trained a fully online, unsupervised model with a redesigned, more informative loss function.

resultF-measure improves by at least 0.1 over IFISTA-ROTAB on LASIESTA and CDNet2014.
